Why I do this work

A short note from me to you.

I got into lending in 2010, right after watching friends and neighbors lose homes to loans nobody had explained to them. I decided then that my clients would always understand exactly what they were signing.

Fifteen years later that is still how I work. I show you every option side by side, I put every cost in writing, and if a loan isn’t right for you I’ll say so, even when it costs me the deal.

I live and work here in California. My clients are my neighbors, and most of them come to me because a friend or their agent told them to call.
